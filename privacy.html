Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who we are

LSTN is operated by LSTN, 103 Corporation Street, Manchester, United Kingdom. Contact: app.lstn@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the information described here.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and display name; a profile photo if you add one.
  • Club activity: clubs you join, picks, ratings, reviews, chat messages, poll votes and event RSVPs.
  • Spotify (only if you link it): your Spotify display name and which tracks of each pick you've played. To check this, LSTN keeps a log of your last 5,000 Spotify plays (song, artist, time) on your device only. It is never uploaded, and it's erased when you disconnect Spotify or sign out. You can optionally import older plays from your Spotify data download. Spotify sign-in tokens stay on your device.
  • Location (only if you tap "Use my location"): your approximate location is used once to search for nearby concerts. It isn't stored.
  • Reports and blocks you make, so we can moderate the community.

How we use it

To run your clubs and show your activity to members of the clubs you join, verify listening, send reminders, suggest albums, find concerts, keep LSTN safe, and provide support. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

AI recommendations

If you allow personal picks, we send your favourite genres, albums you've rated and (if linked) your Spotify top artists and tracks to Anthropic (Claude) to generate suggestions. We never send your name, email or messages. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. You can switch this off in Profile → Settings.

Who we share it with

Service providers who process data for us: Supabase (database and sign-in hosting), Apple (Sign in with Apple, notifications), Spotify (if you link it), Anthropic (AI picks, if allowed) and Ticketmaster (concert search — only artist names and approximate location are sent). Other members of your clubs can see your name, photo, ratings (after each week ends), reviews and messages.

Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Delete your account any time in Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, ratings, reviews, messages and memberships, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Backups are purged within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK GDPR and EU GDPR), you can access, correct, export or delete your data and object to certain processing. Email us to make a request. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

LSTN isn't intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires) and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We'll update this page and the date above if anything changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
